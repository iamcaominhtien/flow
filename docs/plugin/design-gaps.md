# Design gaps

Where each Flow type differs from its page in `docs/design/` (the frozen design). Written by reading the design pages, the skill docs and the code, and by putting a render of one example next to the design's complete example. The plan to close the gaps is phased: foundations, flow-like types, structure types, UML, planning, explainer and wireframe.

Status: **missing** = not built, **partial** = built but not to the page, **checked** = looked at in a picture.

## Foundations

Status: **built** in this phase, with the decisions below. Not checked in the real draw.io editor (only in an approximate viewer).

- **Themes.** `theme` is read for every spec type (`pastel` default, `technical`, `midnight`; unknown name is a `SpecError`), kept in the scene (`scene.json` key `theme`, written only when it is not the default) and used by HTML and draw.io (page background, shape colours, radius, stroke, shadow, rules, symbol-head fill). The themes are overrides on the default in `theme.json`. Layout does not depend on the theme, so one layout serves all three. *Technical* turns the animated roles solid and still ("solid edges"); *Midnight* has its own tints, outlines, line colours and chip text colours, with ink on every fill at 4.5:1 or more (a test). A scene imported from draw.io is always `pastel`.
- **Pastel radius.** The design says 12; the plugin has always drawn 10 and keeps it so the default output does not change.
- **Shadow.** Design "shadow on" is written as `shadow=1` on the rounded boxes in draw.io. The HTML page keeps no shadow on plain boxes (default look unchanged), so the two differ here.
- **Line weights.** One table in `theme.json` (`drawio_weights`): canvas 1, 2, 4 px become draw.io `strokeWidth` 1, 2, 3, so next, yes, no and back lines stay 2 and a canvas 4 becomes 3. The animated tiers take the recipe of design 01 section 6.3: main route `strokeWidth=3`, `flowAnimationDuration=500`; event stream `strokeWidth=1`, `flowAnimationDuration=1500`. Before, the draw.io durations were computed from the HTML cycle (about 620 and 2400 ms) and widths were 4 and 2. The HTML weights (2, 4, 2) are unchanged. Dash patterns are written in multiples of the new draw.io width. The doc's base edge styles use 1.5; the plugin keeps 2 for static lines as the role table says.
- **Type scale.** Written once in `theme.json` (`type_scale`): title Inter 800 20, pill 700 13, node 600 12, edge label 500 10, mono 500 11. Fixed deviations: the title was 24/600, the frame pill 12, edge chips 11/700, the node default 13, key chips 9 (now 10). Nothing under 10 is drawn (a test). The 12 px node text is slightly smaller than before, so the default output is not pixel-identical.
- **draw.io styles of design 01 section 3.** Pill title: frame names are now a real pill cell (`rounded=1;arcSize=50;fontStyle=1;fontSize=13`, id `label-frame-<id>`), read back by the importer; before the name was plain text of the container. Container: `dashPattern=4 3` and `strokeWidth=1.5` added. Node: `strokeWidth`, `fontSize=12`, `fontStyle=1`, `shadow=1`; the corner stays an absolute radius (`arcSize=20` = radius 10) rather than `arcSize=12` per cent, which would draw about 7 px. Kept as they were: container tint (`tone.box` with a `tone.pill` outline, the design lists `#EFF8FF` with `#60A5FA`) and the pill tint (`tone.pill` with ink text, the design shows the accent with white text), so the default frames do not change. Callout tag and root node are not drawn as separate blocks by any type (the concept hub is an ellipse, the mind map is not radial yet), so there is no style to check.
- **Border lint.** `check.py` now warns (`crosses-border`, `on-divider`) when a node or a line label straddles a frame border or sits on a divider; activation bars are exempt. It finds real defects in our examples: `web-cloud` (Users on the Region border), `payment-sequence` (a created participant crossing the fragment), `refund-swimlane` (a label on a lane border), `delivery-er-domains` (a label on a domain border). Not fixed here.
- **Contrast.** The pastel violet key chip text (`#5B4BD6` on `#CFC8F7`) is 3.9:1, below 4.5; it is the design's colour and is left.

## C4, cloud and architecture (phase 3b)

Status: **built**, checked in pictures next to the design's complete examples (C4 context, container, component; cloud basic, delivery, network; architecture delivery plain and illustrated, streaming plain). Not checked in the real draw.io editor.

- **Shared routing.** The three types use `diagrams/grid_routes.py` (the DFD router, now general: any grid diagram, frames as line ends, areas to avoid) and `grid_scene.py` / `grid_frames.py`. The first-choice sides of a line are now horizontal when the two boxes are further apart across than down, in units of the pitches; DFD layouts keep the same checks.
- **C4.** Boxes are `c4_box` and `c4_person` nodes (icon, bold name, mono `[Kind: technology]`, one sentence; the new `Node.technology`), 280 x 132, the system of level 1 360 x 150. Levels 1 to 3 are read from the kinds and checked (no mixed levels, one system on level 1, at most 15 boxes in the frame, a protocol on container lines). `opens` names the spec and the box of the level above; neighbours with only an `id` inherit name, kind, technology, sentence, icon and tone. The frame is the DFD `opened` frame, with `Name [Software system]` or `Name [Container]` on the bottom edge (accent pill, the design draws it dark). Columns 520 and rows 270. Tints follow the kinds when the spec gives none. The old spec keys (`zones`, `zone`, `detail`, `style: async`) are gone.
- **Cloud.** New frame variant `network` (see-through tint of the kind colour, solid or dashed by `Frame.is_solid`, pill in the accent colour on the top edge). Frames are drawn around their parts and inner frames (30 px, 40 px to parts in a subnet); parts 196 x 68, rows 255 apart. Refused: a database in a public subnet or DMZ, a line that skips two trust levels, zones with different subnets. Lines avoid the pills. The old keys (`zones`, `resources`) are gone. Not done: the 40 px grid for frames, a minimum width for the edge and site frames, the `label_room` of 90 px as a check.
- **Architecture.** New frame variant `layer` (dark pill) and node shape `wide_card` (name pill, detail, 64 px icon on a 232 x 96 card; drawn as child cells in draw.io and read back). Layers are rows (260 px plain, 240 px cards), a third party is a zone `beside` a layer, a link may start or end on a zone (frame-edge line; the edge has no node id at that end), line kinds call, main, event, copy. The six patterns are a table in the skill, not a spec key. Not done: the optional entry side, a second line between the same two parts, columns chosen by the links (use `at`). The old keys (`kind` on components, `style: async`, `direction`) are gone.

## Explainer (phase 6a)

Status: **built**, compared with Explainer_Layers, _Tree, _Compare, _Concept and _Payment (HTML in a browser, draw.io in the approximate viewer). Not checked in the real draw.io editor; SVG export not tested.

- **New type, not a mode.** `type: "explainer"` (skill `explainer`) with `shape` layers, tree, comparison or concept, or `sections`. The older `layers` (tech stack) and `concept` (ring of parts) stay as they were; their skills point to the explainer for teaching.
- **Nodes and frames.** New shapes `explain_card` (260 x 200: 88 px detailed icon, name pill, two lines of 12.5 px text, 2 px ink border, 16 px corners), `row_card` (700 x 92 with 60 px icon, mono name and `inside` chips; the small branch card and the "reasons" row use 48 px icons and a sans name), `cell`, `pill_label` (comparison labels and the dark `vs`), `remark` (bold remark, closing line, small note) and `section_head`; new frame variant `strip` (band with a pill label). New node fields `chips` and `mono` (written to `scene.json`). Parts are listed once in `explain_parts.py` and drawn by `html_explain.py` and `drawio_explain.py`, so the two outputs agree.
- **Sizes** are in `theme.json` under `explainer`, as two profiles: `standard` (a page of one shape: bands 248 px high and 290 apart, rows 112 / 124 apart, canvas 1500 or 1400) and `compact` (a section beside others, read from the payment picture: 256 x 196 cards, bands 245 high and 267 apart, comparison rows 103 apart). Title 40 px bold and subtitle 16 px at the top left with 64 px margin, in HTML and draw.io.
- **Numbers.** Cards are numbered in reading order (band, then column, then stack); `number` overrides (`3a`, `6b`); `numbers: false` for none. A tree and a comparison have none. Concept facts are numbered 1 to 4 in reading order (left to right, top to bottom, as in the picture; the design text says clockwise).
- **Lines.** Blue animated `path`, ink `step`, dashed `return`, green `yes`, red `no`; at most two bends; the words of a vertical line sit beside it and in the gap between two bands. Animation only on the path; off for reduced motion; draw.io `flowAnimation` on the path.
- **Warnings.** More than eight cards on a shape or section, more than 14 words on a card, a protocol name (HTTP, TCP, DNS, ...) in the words on a line, no title. Titles are not checked for being a question.
- **Differences from the pictures.** The band label pill, card icon and text sit within a few pixels of the pictures; the free-half remark of a band starts 100 px after the last card rather than on a fixed column; the payment comparison starts 16 px higher than the layered flow beside it; section letters are placed by the same rule for every section. Chips on lines use the plain chip style (the picture's are slightly bolder).
- **Import.** A draw.io file written by Flow is read back with its explainer shapes, text, numbers, chips and bands; icons are not read back (as for every type).
- **Not done.** A card that links to the page that shows it in detail (the design has not decided it). A band with two rows of full cards.
