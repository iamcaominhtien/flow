Orientation and tablet
Rotating a phone changes the screen but not the place in the app, so it is a transition between two frames of the same ID. A tablet in landscape often shows two panes at once, where portrait needs two screens for the same action.

Landscape: master and detail. Tapping a message updates the right pane in place. No arrow and no new screen.
Portrait: two screens. There is no room for two panes, so the same tap opens a separate detail screen, with a back arrow.
Drawing orientation and tabletsSame ID, new orientation. The two phone frames are S40. A rotate chip (slate, with a phone icon) links them.Frame sizes follow the Rules artboard: phone 156 × 324, tablet 164 × 236 (portrait) and 236 × 164 (landscape), drawn at their own scales.Different structure per orientation gets a different ID: S50 is one master-detail screen, S50 and S51 are the portrait pair.
