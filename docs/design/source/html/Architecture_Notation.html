Notation: components, lines, zones
How to read one box, one line and one frame. The same pieces are used in every architecture diagram.

Plain
196 × 68. Flat icon, name, one line of detail.
Illustrated
232 × 96. Name pill, one line of detail, 64 px icon.
Name
What it is, in the team's words: Order service, Orders database. Two or three words.
Detail line
The technology or the job, one short line: PostgreSQL, push and SMS. Never a sentence.
Icon
From the same sets as the other diagram types. Pick by the noun: Database, Queue, Gateway.
Tint
Groups parts by domain or owner, not by layer. Courier is teal everywhere.
Lines
Call or request
A synchronous call. Ink, thin. Label the protocol or the action: HTTPS, SQL, charge.
Main path
Blue, thick, animated fast. The one route the diagram is about. One per diagram.
Event or message
Violet, thin, animated slowly. Asynchronous: queues, topics, streams.
Optional or copy
Dashed slate. Replication, a fallback, or a return you want to show.
A label sits on the line, a verb or a protocol, 90 px of room at least.
Arrows show who starts the call, not which way the data flows.

Frames nest, with 30 px between an outer and an inner edge.
Label is a dark pill on the top left edge. One frame, one word or two.
A part belongs to the smallest frame that holds it.
A line from a frame edge means "any part inside" (one line, one label).
Layer tints: clients blue, edge violet, services green, data amber,
third party grey. Region and network frames take blue and amber.
