Usage rules

Show at 32–48 px in diagrams, never below 24. Place on white or a light tint, not on dark backgrounds. One icon style per diagram. The label always carries the meaning; the icon only names the kind of thing. Accent colors follow the foundation: blue for compute and process, green for success and data, amber for storage and warmth, violet for services, red only for warning or stop.
