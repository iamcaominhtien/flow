# screen-L4-A-01: second run, a Sonnet agent, plugin 0.16.0

Brief: `benchmarks/briefs/screen-l4.md`. One fresh Sonnet agent got the `Brief` text (with the source folder and the address filled in) and the screen-flow skill, with `benchmarks/`, `examples/`, `docs/`, `tests/`, the changelog, the git history and the earlier runs closed. The Angular Conduit app was served from the build of the first run at `http://127.0.0.1:4200`. The agent was asked to run the drift check as well.

## Result

- **Capture**: 12 desktop screenshots from the running app, from its own plan. It reached pages by address rather than by clicking, because a click left a focus ring in the screenshot.
- **Code**: `flow.py code` on the source, not corrected; the draft (`flow.py draft`) was written and not used.
- **Diagram**: final check **0 errors, 15 warnings** (13 `lines-cross`, 1 `label-on-line`, 1 `lines-overlap`). The first run had 30 warnings, 22 of them crossings. Page about 3300 x 3100 px, every screen at `"size": "large"`.
- **Content**: 12 screens at L4, 3 decisions ("Account created?", "Credentials ok?", and "Signed in?" from the route guard), 2 entry points, 41 taps. The top bar of each group is drawn once: 7 `tab` items on 2 buses (signed out: Home, Sign in, Sign up; signed in: Home, New Article, Settings, own name). Two long returns are jumps (the logout, and Sign in from the article read by a visitor). Seven taps merge into 4 drawn arrows with two triggers each.
- **Drift**: `30 of 41 taps checked`, no `tap-not-in-code`; 18 `link-not-drawn`, 2 `guard-not-drawn` (`/settings` and `/editor/:slug`, entered without a decision), 1 `page-not-drawn` (`/profile/:username/favorites`). The agent listed all of them in `desc`. By its reading, every `link-not-drawn` is a link that shows in another state (Edit for the author, Favorite for a visitor, other people's profiles).
- About 6 minutes.

Read by the lead (the final picture): both zones and their bars, the three decisions in one row under the signed-out pages, the blue main path from the site through Sign in to the signed-in home. The `desc` (918 characters) runs over the first zone's label and the bar's chip.

## Gaps the agent hit, and what changed (0.16.1)

1. `"conditions": "coded"` coded every arrow (C1 to C32); the skill said only long or shared labels were. The skill now says the top-level setting codes every arrow with words, and that a few long ones take `"code"` each.
2. No example of the taps from top bar links to their pages, nor of two bars for two groups: the skill has one now.
3. A long `desc` ran into the diagram, and nothing said so: new check `description-too-long`, measured with Inter's widths and wrapped at word breaks. It fires on this run's `desc` (about 7 lines) and on none of the examples.
4. Found by the lead: with the top bar in its own row inside a zone, that band had no entry in it, so its column 0 started at the left margin and the zone reached over the entry column; the "Entry points" frame was hidden under it. A band with no entry now starts right of the entries' column.
5. Found by the lead: the entry's second line ("typed address or bookmark") wrapped out of its box. Entries, flags and tabs widen to fit the second line too.
6. Open: drift works on pages, not states, so links of one state are reported for every screen of the page. The decisions sit under the pages that feed them and their arrows to the signed-in home run long.
